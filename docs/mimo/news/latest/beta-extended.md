# Notice on the Extension of the Limited-Time Experience of MiMo-V2.5-Pro-UltraSpeed

Thank you all for your attention and support for Xiaomi MiMo-V2.5-Pro-UltraSpeed. **After evaluation by the team, we have decided to extend the limited-time experience window for UltraSpeed to allow more developers to have the opportunity to access and experience the inference capability of 1000 tokens/s.** 

<br></br>

We firmly believe that the ultimate inference speed will bring about brand-new usage scenarios and paradigms in the industry. 

Since the launch of MiMo-V2.5-Pro-UltraSpeed, we have received enthusiastic applications from developers across various industries.

As of June 23, we have received over 66,000 usage applications from numerous Fortune Global 500 companies, leading enterprises in various industries, and individual developers, covering multiple fields such as law, finance, communications, logistics, automobile manufacturing, culture and media, and higher education institutions, far exceeding our expectations. 

<br></br>

Seeing the urgent and genuine demand from the community for MiMo-V2.5-Pro-UltraSpeed, we hope to provide more users with the opportunity to experience the paradigm shift brought by 1000 tokens/s, so we have decided **to extend the duration of the chat experience and API access experience for MiMo-V2.5-Pro-UltraSpeed**. 

After this extension, you can continue to apply for the closed beta of MiMo-V2.5-Pro-UltraSpeed, and users who have been approved can continue to use it. 

The specific offline time will be arranged separately based on resource availability, and we will inform you in advance to ensure that everyone has sufficient time to prepare for migration and adaptation.
