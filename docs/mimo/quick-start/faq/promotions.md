# Promotions

### What promotions and benefits are currently available?

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}
The main benefit currently offered is bonus credit, which can be used to offset pay-as-you-go API usage and quickly try MiMo models. You can receive bonus credit through **Refer & Earn** or new user registration. Eligibility, credit amounts, and payout rules vary by campaign. Bonus credit is currently valid for 40 days and expires after that.

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}

### How do I join "Refer & Earn"?

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}
Any registered user can invite; new users (signed up **within 3 days**) can be invited. After your friend enters via your invite link and the code is bound, you both instantly get **$2 in credits**. When your friend completes their first paid order, you earn an additional **10% of their paid amount** in credits and they get **10% off**.

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}

### Where is my invite code? How do I share it?

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}
After signing in, use the "**Invite friends**" feature to view your **6-character invite code**. You can copy the invite link or save the invite poster to share with friends.

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}

### How does my friend redeem the code?

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}
After your friend clicks your invite link and signs in to the console, **the system will automatically bind the invite code** (no manual entry required). Once the binding succeeds, the $2 credits are credited instantly.

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}
If the invite code is not filled in automatically after opening the link, your friend can manually enter the 6-character code in the console to bind. Each invitee can redeem only **1** invite code.

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}

### Does my friend have to pay for me to earn rewards? How do the two tiers relate?

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}
No. Redeeming the code instantly triggers **Tier 1 (Signup credits)** — you both get $2 in credits, whether or not they pay. If your friend later completes a first paid order, **Tier 2 (Credits back)** kicks in — you earn 10% of their paid amount. **The two tiers are independent and can both be earned.** 

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}

### What counts as a "first paid order"?

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}
A "first paid order" is your friend's **first paid Token Plan subscription** within the lifetime of their account.

- **Includes:** Token Plan subscription first payment

- **Does not include:** API top-ups

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}
**30-day first-order window:** Your friend must complete their first paid order **within 30 days of binding the invite code**; after 30 days, credits back will no longer be issued (**signup reward is unaffected**). The 10% first-order discount follows the same 30-day window: first orders completed after 30 days no longer get the discount.

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}
**Free Token Plan exclusion:** If your friend has already received **1 month or more of free Token Plan**, their first order no longer gets **10% off**, and the purchase does not count toward referral rewards (no credits back will be issued).

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}

### How many friends can I invite at most?

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}
Invite limits are **calculated as three separate caps**:

- **Signup-credit quota: 30 slots** — first 30 bindings, both parties get $2; after 30, both stop receiving $2

- **Bindings: unlimited** — you can keep inviting any number of friends, and they still get **10% off** on their first order

- **Credits-back quota: 30 paid orders** — first 30 paid orders earn 10% credits back

- **Special bonus:** Standard annual, Pro annual, and Max (any duration) members earn **20%** credits back on paid orders, with no 30-order cap.

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}

### What can credits be used for?

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}
**Credits** can only offset Xiaomi MiMo model **API call** charges.

- **Redeemable for:** API call charges

- **Not redeemable for:** Token Plan subscription

- **Non-cashable, non-transferable, no change given**

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}
**Valid for 40 days:** counted from the credited date, earliest-expiring first (FIFO); unused balance after 40 days expires automatically.

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}

### Does my friend's refund affect my rewards?

- **Signup credits:** already-issued credits will not be reversed

- **Credits back (refund within 3-day pending window):** the credits will not be issued

- **Credits back (refund after credited):** the unused portion of the inviter's credits is **immediately voided**

- **Refunds do not affect counts:** the 30-paid-order cap is counted by "paid order completed", and counts are not reversed by refunds

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}

### My friend redeemed my code, but I don't see the reward.

{/* feishu-style:text-align:left */}
Check in order:

1. **Confirm the binding succeeded:** once the binding succeeds, you and your friend each instantly get $2 in credits

1. **Signup credits should arrive instantly:** the system may have a delay — please wait a moment and refresh the page

1. **Credits back arrive 3 days after the friend's first paid order:** please wait 3 days

1. **Check the 30-day first-order window:** your friend must complete the first paid order within 30 days of binding; after 30 days, no credits back are issued

1. **Check the cap:** After 30 successful invites, the ¥10 registration incentive stops. After 30 paid referrals, the 10% bonus stops — unless you are on Standard annual, Pro annual, or Max monthly/annual plan.
